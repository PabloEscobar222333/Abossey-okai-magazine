Trading Guidelines

How buying and selling works on ABBOSSEY OKAI MAGAZINE, and the rules every merchant and buyer agrees to.

ABBOSSEY OKAI MAGAZINE is a discovery platform, not a shop. We list parts and accessories from physical stalls in Abossey Okai, Accra, and connect you to the dealer on WhatsApp or with directions. Payment and delivery are agreed directly between buyer and dealer.

For buyers

  1. Confirm before you pay. Chat with the dealer on WhatsApp, ask for a live photo or video of the exact part, and confirm the price, condition (New, Used or Refurbished) and vehicle fitment.
  2. Prefer paying at the stall. Every verified dealer has a physical stall and GPS pin. Use "Locate Merchant Shop" to visit, inspect the part and pay on the spot where you can.
  3. Be careful with advance payments. Never send a deposit to a personal mobile money number that doesn't match the shop's listed WhatsApp number. If a dealer insists on full payment upfront from an unknown number, stop and report it.
  4. Check the part number. Compare the OEM or part number on the item with your old part or your vehicle's manual before you accept it.
  5. Agree on returns up front. Ask the dealer about their return or exchange policy before paying, and keep your receipt or WhatsApp chat as proof.
  6. Report anything suspicious. Use Report Scam Listings if a listing is fake, a dealer asks for money dishonestly, or the part delivered was not what was listed.

For merchants

  1. List only what you actually stock. Mark items "Out of Stock" or hide them as soon as they sell. Repeatedly listing parts you don't have leads to takedowns.
  2. Use real photos and honest descriptions. Photograph the actual item in your stall. No stolen images, misleading brand names or "genuine" claims for aftermarket parts.
  3. State the right condition and price. Choose New, Used or Refurbished truthfully, and quote the price buyers will really pay. Bait pricing is not allowed.
  4. Get the fitment right. Only tag vehicles the part really fits. Buyers report wrong fitment, and repeated reports affect your Verified status.
  5. Respond professionally. Reply to WhatsApp leads promptly, be polite and never pressure buyers to pay before they have seen the part.
  6. Keep your shop details current. Your WhatsApp number, stall location and map pin must be accurate so buyers can find you. Update them from your Merchant Portal.
  7. One account per shop. Accounts are created by our admin team after a stall visit. Do not share your PIN or lend your account to another seller.

Not allowed on the platform

  • Counterfeit parts sold as genuine, or parts with falsified brand markings.
  • Stolen vehicle parts, or parts with chassis, engine or serial numbers removed.
  • Safety-critical parts (brakes, airbags, seat belts, steering) that are damaged, expired or salvaged and sold as safe.
  • Listings for anything other than vehicle parts and accessories.
  • Fake reviews, duplicate listings to crowd search results, or impersonating another shop.
  • Requesting payment outside the dealer's listed shop number, or any form of advance-fee fraud.

How we enforce these rules

1
Warning

First confirmed issue: we message the dealer on WhatsApp and give 48 hours to correct the listing.

2
Takedown

Listings that stay misleading, or any counterfeit or scam listing, are removed by our moderators.

3
Suspension

Repeat offences or fraud lead to loss of Verified status and suspension of the shop account.
